I served as a **Conference Supporting Student** at the [14th International Conference on Indoor Positioning and Indoor Navigation (IPIN 2024)](https://ipin-conference.org/2024/), held in Hong Kong on **14–17 October 2024**. The conference was organized by the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.
